---
title: Design system
description: Public theme-author tokens and interaction rules.
---

OpenBoxGL uses blue-black surfaces, cool text, cyan focus and selection, green launch actions, and orange lifecycle accents. The application source of truth is `DESIGN.md`.

Use layered surfaces, restrained shadows, small radii, readable labels, and visible focus states. Cover art is the main browsing surface; metadata stays near the selected game. Stock themes may change palette, typography, and surface treatment while preserving the interaction structure.

Do not use permanent glow, deep shadows on every component, or low-contrast text.
